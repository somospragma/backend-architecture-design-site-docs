A **community** artifact is created by a Pragma member in their personal account, is open source and is useful to others. Pragma doesn't own it: its owners do.

## Requirements

- Owners identified by GitHub username.
- An SPDX-recognized open source license, such as `MIT` or `Apache-2.0`.
- The same [entry criteria](../contribute/#entry-criteria) as any artifact.

## From community to official

An artifact becomes official when:

1. A Pragma team is willing to maintain it.
2. The repository is transferred to a Pragma account, or there's an agreement on where it will live.
3. A PR changes `origin: community` to `origin: official` and updates the owners.

The entry approvers confirm the change in that PR.
